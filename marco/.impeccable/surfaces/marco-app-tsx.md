# Marco Roofing pitch surface

- Scope: the standalone `/marco-roofing/` GitHub Pages concept.
- Mode: Persuade.
- Audience: Bay Area homeowners evaluating a roofer, plus the Marco Roofing owner evaluating a replacement website.
- Job: establish trust quickly, make verified services and coverage legible, and keep direct calling effortless.
- Primary action: call Marco Roofing; secondary action: preview the non-connected quote-request flow.
- Proof: public 4.9 rating, established 1975, Diamond Certified profile, public license record, recent attributed review summaries, Fremont address and hours.
- Constraints: clearly independent and uncommissioned; no form transmission or storage; illustrative imagery must be disclosed; do not imply the current redirect's cause.
- Direction: review-led reputation page inside the roofing-material sample-book visual system.
- Memorable moment: the first viewport pairs the oversized Bay Area promise with a roof plane and a compact 4.9 rating plate.
- Unresolved: final owner-approved copy, real project photography, form-delivery destination, and live review integration.
