What they do

Roof work, start to finish.

The services below are drawn from the company’s own Google listing and from work customers describe in their reviews.

  • Roof repair

    Targeted repair of the section that is failing — lifted or missing shingles, worn valleys, flashing at walls, vents and skylights.

  • Leak detection

    Tracing a ceiling stain back to where water is actually entering, which is rarely directly above the stain.

  • Storm & wind damage

    The most common job in the reviews. Includes documenting the damage and working through the insurance claim.

  • Full replacement & reroof

    Tear-off, underlayment, new roof system, shingle colour selection, and a walkthrough at the end.

  • Gutters & gutter guards

    Gutter installation and gutter protection, frequently paired with fascia and soffit work in the same visit.

  • Roof inspection

    A practical look at condition and remaining life — what needs attention now and what is fine for another season.

  • Attic & loft ventilation

    Venting the roof properly, including running bathroom fans out through the roof rather than into the attic.

  • Preventative maintenance

    Keeping a sound roof sound, which is considerably cheaper than replacing one that has been left alone.

Not sure which of these you need? That is what the first phone call is for.

What happens next

From phone call to finished roof.

Assembled from the sequence customers describe in their own reviews.

  1. 01

    The call

    Describe what changed — a stain, a drip, a wind event, missing material. Where it is and when it started matters more than technical vocabulary.

  2. 02

    Inspection

    Someone comes out and looks properly, including the parts you cannot see from the ground: valleys, flashing, penetrations, ventilation.

  3. 03

    Scope & estimate

    Materials and options explained in plain terms — shingle choices, underlayment, what holds up in San Jose sun. Insurance coordinated where a claim applies.

  4. 04

    The work

    Scheduled around you, with a named contact for updates. Neighbours notified, access confirmed, the site kept tidy day to day.

  5. 05

    Walkthrough

    You are invited up or around to inspect the finished work with the crew before they leave — then a final cleanup pass with magnet rollers.
