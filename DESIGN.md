# TopGuard Roofing Visual System

## Direction

A roofing material sample book translated into a modern local-service website. The page should feel precise, tactile, and assembled, with every section acting like another protective layer.

## Mode and Dials

- Mode: Persuade
- Design variance: 6
- Motion intensity: 4
- Visual density: 4

## Palette

- Paper: `#F5F2EA`
- Ink: `#17201F`
- Muted ink: `#52605D`
- Roof: `#293432`
- Line: `#CBD0C8`
- Action orange: `#E95B24`
- Action orange dark: `#B83A10`

The page remains light throughout. Orange is the only accent and always indicates action or a meaningful highlight.

## Typography

- Display: IBM Plex Sans Condensed, strong and architectural.
- Body: Source Sans 3, open and practical.
- Headlines are compact, left aligned, and never use decorative eyebrows.

## Shape and Depth

- Corners use 2px or 14px depending on whether the object is structural or interactive.
- Borders carry the sample-book structure. Shadows are soft, offset, and reserved for photography or raised controls.

## Photography

Natural, credible California residential roofing photography. Never present generated photography as completed TopGuard project work. Use it as illustrative demo imagery until the owner supplies a gallery.

## Interaction

- The hero image reveals with a single roof-plane clip animation.
- Service rows respond like selected material swatches.
- Buttons move by one pixel and deepen their shadow on hover.
- The quote form provides clear validation and a truthful demo success state.

## Responsive Rules

- Desktop uses an asymmetric, edge-to-edge hero and an indexed service layout.
- Mobile stacks all content, preserves large tap targets, and adds a persistent call-and-quote action bar.
- Reduced-motion users receive the complete static composition with no reveal animation.
