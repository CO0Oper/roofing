What we do

Six kinds of roof work,
one standard of finish.

A roof is a system — shingles, flashing, ventilation, drainage and the details around every penetration. We price and build all of it as one job.

01

Roof replacement

Full tear-off and new roofing system — asphalt shingle, metal, or flat EPDM with added ISO insulation. Shingle samples brought to your door, color matched to the house and the San Jose sun.

  • Asphalt & architectural shingle
  • Standing-seam & aluminum metal
  • Flat / low-slope EPDM
02

Leak repair & detection

Water rarely enters where it shows up. We inspect the roof and the attic, trace the actual path, and fix the cause — missed flashing, rotted decking, a bad prior repair — without pushing a full reroof you don’t need.

  • Attic + roof-deck inspection
  • Flashing & penetration rebuilds
  • Decking & underlayment repair
03

Storm & wind damage

Missing shingles after a windy night, or a full claim after a major storm. Small repairs get taken seriously, and we’ll work directly with your insurance company when the damage warrants it.

  • Same-day shingle repairs
  • Documented damage reports
  • Insurance coordination
04

Gutters & downspouts

Roof drainage that actually moves water away from the foundation. New gutter systems, downspout re-routing, and repairs when a moving truck finds the one above your garage.

  • Full gutter replacement
  • Downspout & runoff re-routing
  • Foundation drainage fixes
05

Skylights & flashing

The parts of a roof that leak first. New and custom-sourced skylights, curb flashing rebuilt properly, and drop cloths inside the house so the hallway isn’t full of debris afterwards.

  • Skylight replacement & sourcing
  • Curb & step flashing
  • Solar panel take-off & reset
06

Attic venting & insulation

A roof that can’t breathe cooks itself from underneath. We balance intake and exhaust venting and add insulation where the assembly needs it — especially on flat and low-slope roofs.

  • Ridge, soffit & static venting
  • ISO insulation board
  • HVAC curb & penetration sealing

Skylights, chimneys, valleys, vent stacks, HVAC curbs, roof-to-wall transitions — that’s where nearly every leak we get called out to fix actually starts. Usually because someone before us treated those details as an afterthought.

Questions

The things homeowners ask first.

How fast can you get out here for a leak?

The listing is open 24 hours and emergency patches are part of what we do — one recent review describes a same-evening shingle repair after wind damage, and another an emergency patch handled before a full replacement was scheduled. Call or text and we’ll tell you honestly when we can be there.

Do I actually need a whole new roof?

Often, no. Several reviews describe us fixing the real problem — missed flashing, a bad prior repair, rotted decking — without a full tear-off. We inspect the attic as well as the roof so the recommendation is based on what’s actually failing, not on what’s easiest to sell.

Do you work with insurance after a storm?

Yes. We document the damage and coordinate with the insurance company when a claim is warranted. What we don’t do is pressure homeowners into filing one — a review specifically calls out that we offered honest advice and fair out-of-pocket pricing instead of pushing a claim.

Is financing available?

Yes — financing has been offered to homeowners who wanted that option. Ask about it during the estimate and we’ll walk through what’s available for your project.

How long does a reroof take?

Many single-family asphalt shingle reroofs finish in a single day. Larger jobs, flat roofs with insulation work, metal roofing, or projects that include gutters and siding run longer — you’ll get the real timeline in writing before anything starts.

What kinds of roofs do you install?

Asphalt and architectural shingle, metal and aluminum, and flat / low-slope EPDM systems with ISO insulation board. We also handle skylights, solar panel take-off and reset, gutters, downspouts, siding and attic ventilation as part of the same project.

Will my yard be a mess afterwards?

Cleanup comes up in nearly every review we have. Landscaping gets protected, debris is hauled off, and the yard gets swept for nails. For interior work like skylight replacement, drop cloths go down inside the house.

Which areas do you serve?

San Jose and the surrounding South Bay — including Willow Glen, Almaden, Cambrian, Campbell, Los Gatos and Santa Clara. If you’re nearby but not sure, just call and ask.
