- The business owner, evaluating the website as a direct lead and call channel.

## Constraints

- Do not invent licensing, warranties, financing, certifications, years in business, response times, staff biographies, or completed projects.
- Generated photography is illustrative and must not be represented as company project work.
- The pitch form does not send or store data.
- Final services, facts, photography, and form delivery require owner approval.

## Accessibility

Semantic HTML, visible focus, high contrast, reduced-motion support, useful labels, and a persistent mobile contact bar.
