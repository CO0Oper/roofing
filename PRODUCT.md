# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React with Vite and TypeScript, chosen for a fast, portable pitch demo that can be deployed as a static site and extended with a form backend later.

## Users

- Primary: San Jose homeowners who need roof inspection, repair, replacement, gutter, flashing, skylight, ventilation, or related roofing work.
- Secondary: The owner of TopGuard Roofing, evaluating the website as a sales and lead-generation tool.

## Product Purpose

Give prospective customers enough confidence to call or request a quote, while demonstrating to the owner how a professional website could turn existing Google visibility into direct leads.

## Positioning

The site turns TopGuard Roofing's existing local reputation and detailed customer feedback into a direct, mobile-friendly path from trust to contact.

## Operating Context

Most visitors will arrive on a phone while researching a roofing problem, comparing contractors, or responding to storm or wind damage. They need quick access to services, proof, service-area information, and a phone number.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Display the verified business name, phone number, San Jose address, veteran-owned designation, rating, and review count shown in the supplied Google listing screenshots.
- Offer tap-to-call and a quote-request form for new customers.
- The pitch-demo form must not imply that it sends or stores real customer data; production delivery remains an integration decision for the owner.
- Use selected excerpts from the supplied Google reviews as temporary demo content.
- A production review integration must preserve Google attribution and direct-source links where required.
- Do not invent licenses, warranties, financing, response times, years in business, team biographies, certifications, or project photography.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: TopGuard Roofing.
- Voice: capable, straightforward, local, respectful, and free of exaggerated sales claims.
- The listing identifies the company as veteran-owned.

## Evidence on Hand

- Google listing screenshot: 5.0 rating from 21 reviews, roofing contractor, open 24 hours, phone +1 669-323-7770, 4144 Casa Grande Way, San Jose, CA 95118, and veteran-owned designation.
- Five supplied customer-review screenshots naming roofing services and describing workmanship, communication, cleanup, and value.
- No confirmed logo, website, social accounts, owner email, license number, warranty terms, project gallery, or form-delivery destination.

## Product Principles

- Put the phone number and quote path within immediate reach on mobile.
- Earn trust with specific customer evidence, not unsupported claims.
- Make urgent roofing needs easy to identify and act on.
- Keep the demo honest about what is and is not yet connected.
- Make the site feel established without pretending the business supplied assets it has not supplied.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, strong contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, descriptive labels, and layouts that remain usable at narrow mobile widths.
